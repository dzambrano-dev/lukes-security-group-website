About Lukes Security Group

Lukes Security Group is a family-owned and operated security company built on professionalism, discretion, and real-world experience in nightlife, private events, VIP environments, and business security.

Founder / Owner

Kevin Lukes

Kevin Lukes leads by example and is often on the front lines alongside his team, ensuring guards are informed, prepared, and ready to meet each client’s needs. Built on strong family values, Kevin has shaped Lukes Security Group around professionalism, reliability, and respect.

Director of Operations

Andrew “Doobie” Dueber

Andrew “Doobie” Dueber serves as a primary point of contact for clients, helping coordinate security coverage based on each event’s needs. He organizes guard assignments, communicates shift details, and helps ensure the right team is sent to the right location.
